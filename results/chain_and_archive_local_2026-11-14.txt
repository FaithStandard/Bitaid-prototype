Seven suites, BITAID_CHECKPOINT_BLOCKS=1. No failed check in any.

What this build adds over anchored_timing_local_2026-11-14:
- attending ballots (commitment and reveal) count from the block that anchors them, within windows measured from the incident's block; a posting disputes the vote only within its escalation window
- the incident transaction records its case (INCIDENT); the chain shows the pool's signature in its witness; a posting counts only for a case with such a transaction earlier on the chain
- each tier commitment is a ledger record (TIER); archivists keep and serve each case's signed log; a node reads the logs of tiers it holds no messages for, checks and replays them
