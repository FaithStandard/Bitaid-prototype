Bitaid prototype: waits that fit Tor, tested under simulated Tor lag, local regtest, 2026-11-16

Why: two public signet runs over Tor (2026-11-15) failed on waits tuned on a local machine (20 s for
nonce reservations, 30 s for acceptances), though every party was answering.

What changed:
- one floor under every wait on a reply (BITAID_REPLY_MIN: 900 s on Tor runs); a wait still ends the moment the reply comes
- the joining window counts from the first acceptance (Specification §5), at least three times that acceptance's delay
- a stalling co-signer costs a session three times the honest members' answer time, not the floor
- BITAID_LAG delays every received message (here 0.15-1.8 s, 1 in 20 by 13 s, with 3 s blocks: a spike spans about four blocks)
- found under lag and fixed: rosters arriving out of order; refund heights judged against a moving tip; a member attesting while an appeal
  was still unanchored or in flight; a tier's commitment refused when the next tier's appeal came first; a replacement seat's
  commitment dropped when it came before its drawing; the caller's and responders' waits for nonces, roster, incident and outcome
